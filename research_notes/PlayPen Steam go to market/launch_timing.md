# Steam Launch Timing for Two Staggered Corrupted Media Launches (Grenade Junky PC VR, then PlayPen), Late 2026 to 2027

Research date: 2026-10-19. "ANNOUNCED" = on Valve's Steamworks Upcoming Events page or Valve's published calendar. "INFERRED" = projected from past-year patterns and not yet announced.

## 1. Official Steam event calendar, late 2026 to 2027, and the rules for launching around events

### Takeaway
Valve has announced everything through July 8, 2027. H2 2027 (Autumn Sale, October Next Fest, Steam Scream VI, Winter Sale 2027) is not announced yet. The calendar for H1 2027 has no VR, puzzle or platformer fest. The rules that matter most: (a) a new release can't be discounted for 30 days, so it can't join a seasonal sale that starts within 30 days of launch; (b) the launch discount runs 7 to 14 days at up to 40%; (c) only unreleased games can enter Next Fest, and each game gets one Next Fest ever.

**2026 pattern, for inferring H2 2027.** Autumn Sale moved to **Oct 1 to 8, 2026** (it used to be late November). Cooking Fest ran Oct 12 to 19, then Next Fest Oct 19 to 26, then Steam Scream V Oct 26 to Nov 2. The 2026 calendar had no VR, puzzle or platformer fest. **Discount and launch rules** — [Steamworks: Discounting](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/discounts)
- Launch discount: optional, set before release, runs 7 to 14 days, 10% to 40% off (Valve suggests 10% to 15%).
- 30-day cooldown between discounts. Seasonal sales are exempt from it.
- A product can't be discounted for 30 days after release (the "Release Cooldown"), so a game that launches within 30 days of a seasonal sale can't join that sale until the cooldown ends. It can join mid-sale once the cooldown clears.
- No price increase within 30 days of release. A price increase triggers a 30-day discount cooldown. Base price can't change while a discount is active or scheduled.
- Seasonal sale eligibility: released at least 30 days before the event starts — [Steamworks: Upcoming Events](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events)
- Themed fests each have their own eligibility. Discounted games get priority, but Coming Soon titles and titles with demos can also take part. Registration is required — [Steamworks: Upcoming Events](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events)

**Next Fest eligibility** — [Steamworks: Next Fest](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest)
- Only unreleased games qualify. A game that releases before or during an edition is out of that edition. The game must not release before the edition ends; any date after it is fine.
- Each title gets **one Next Fest only**. Prologues, Chapter Ones and re-releases of existing Steam games are ineligible.
- Early Access and F2P launches count as unreleased until they launch.
- The base game store page must be public. A public demo must be live before the fest starts.
- Valve recommends keeping the demo up after the fest and running no Playtest during it.

**Practical tactic (opinion).** Launch about one week before a seasonal sale and ask Valve to extend your launch discount into the sale. The author says Valve usually grants this only to top-tier games with tens of thousands of wishlists — [howtomarketagame: best day to release](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/02/14/whats-the-best-day-to-release-an-indie-game/)

## 2. Day-of-week and month-of-year effects, AAA competition, release volume

### Takeaway
Launch on a Monday, Wednesday or Thursday. Saturday and Sunday are blocked, and Tuesday is Steam maintenance day. January to April is the least crowded period for launches. October to December has about 30% more launches than January. Seasonal sales and Next Fest weeks shrink the front-page new-release widgets. AAA launches matter far less than indies assume. GTA VI is dated Nov 19, 2026 on console only, so its effect on Steam is minimal.

### Cited Findings
- Valve blocks weekend launch dates (support is unstaffed). Avoid Tuesday (maintenance). Monday or Wednesday is suggested: a Monday launch that reaches Popular Upcoming collects wishlists over the weekend before it — [howtomarketagame](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/02/14/whats-the-best-day-to-release-an-indie-game/)
- Popular Upcoming has 10 slots ranked by release date. Eligibility takes roughly 6,000 to 7,000 wishlists. Fewer eligible games in your week means a longer stay on the widget — [howtomarketagame](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/02/14/whats-the-best-day-to-release-an-indie-game/)
- January to April has the fewest launches. June and July dip slightly. October to December has about 30% more releases than January. Simon Carless's wishlist-to-sales analysis rates Jan to Apr "good", June "very good", and Oct to Dec "less good, though not by much" — [howtomarketagame](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/02/14/whats-the-best-day-to-release-an-indie-game/)
- Steamdata's revenue index for indies under $20, indexed to August = 100: Sep 78, Feb 72, Jan 38. Secondary analytics blog, so treat as directional — [steamdata.ai](https://steamdata.ai/en-US/blog/best-month-to-launch-game-on-steam) (via search summary)
- Seasonal sales and Next Fest reduce the front-page new-release widgets — [howtomarketagame](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/02/14/whats-the-best-day-to-release-an-indie-game/). Big sales rearrange the front page. Avoid the first days of Next Fest because streamers are busy with demos. Most games launch between 6 and 10am Pacific — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/whats-the-best-timing-to-release)
- **Contrarian evidence (Feb 2026):** Zukowski scraped the front page during January 2026, around and just after the Winter Sale. Games stayed on Popular Upcoming longer because few titles launched, so nothing pushed them off.
  - Popular Upcoming: 163 games, average 2.1 days on the list.
  - New & Trending (paid): 95 games, average 4.25 days.
  - How Many Dudes launched its demo during the Winter Sale and went from 8,387 to 150,292 wishlists between Dec 18 and Jan 31.
  - Zukowski's argument: herding away from sales, Next Fest and AAA weeks creates gaps. Caveat: one month of data.
  - [howtomarketagame, Feb 2026](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/02/18/the-counterintuitive-advantage-of-launching-during-steams-busiest-event/)
- AAA: Monster Hunter Rise peaked at about 130k concurrent players at launch, around 1.6% of Steam users that day. Carless reportedly found big AAA releases don't affect indie sales — [howtomarketagame](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/02/14/whats-the-best-day-to-release-an-indie-game/). Counterpoint: the "Cyberpunk avoidance zone" — in Cyberpunk 2077's launch week, only 2 games with more than 1,000 Steam followers launched — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/whats-the-best-timing-to-release)
- GTA VI: Nov 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series. A PC version is expected later — [PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/3052217/gta-6s-release-date-confirmed-for-real-this-time-seriously.html); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/gta-6-release-date)
- Other 2027 heavyweights reported, all provisional:
  - Metro 2039: Feb 2027
  - A new God of War: early 2027, unconfirmed
  - Final Fantasy VII Revelation: spring 2027
  - The Expanse: Osiris Reborn: Q2 2027
  - Fable and Resident Evil titles: 2027
  - Publishers reportedly clustered big releases away from the GTA VI window, which crowds late 2026 and early 2027.
  - [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/life-after-gta-6-2027s-biggest-video-game-releases-are-coming)
- Release volume:
  - 2025: about 19,008 releases per SteamDB (Dec 2025), up from 18,558 in 2024 — [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/12/over-19000-games-have-released-on-steam-in-2025-with-nearly-half-seeing-fewer-than-10-reviews/page=1/). Other trackers put it at about 20,000 to 20,500 (Zukowski: 20,282, about 55 a day) — [howtomarketagame](https://howtomarketagame.com/?p=5565)
  - Nearly half of 2025 releases got fewer than 10 reviews — [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/12/over-19000-games-have-released-on-steam-in-2025-with-nearly-half-seeing-fewer-than-10-reviews/page=1/)
  - H1 2025: 9,517 releases, with March the peak month at 1,817 — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-48224)
  - March 2026 was reported as an all-time monthly record: 2,227 per WN Hub, 2,467 per another source citing SteamDB. Different trackers — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-50478)

### Inferences
- For a $10 VR title or a small 2D indie, AAA weeks are close to irrelevant. GTA VI is console-only at launch and won't touch Steam traffic.
- The real competitors are other indies with enough wishlists to reach Popular Upcoming. Check SteamDB's upcoming list 2 to 3 weeks out and pick the emptiest Monday or Wednesday.
- Rough cost of being in a sale or Next Fest week: fewer slots, but also fewer competing launches. For a game below the Popular Upcoming threshold (about 6 to 7k wishlists), the main cost of launching inside a seasonal sale is losing eligibility for that sale, not visibility.
- March is now the busiest month by release count (it holds both a Next Fest wrap-up and the Spring Sale). "January to April is quiet" is an older pattern and may be fading. Treat early March as crowded.

### Gaps
- No authoritative month-by-month 2025 or 2026 release counts from SteamDB. Only partial numbers that conflict between sources.
- No current dataset on day-of-week revenue outcomes. The evidence is anecdotal or from 2021 to 2023.

## 3. Coming Soon page lead time, Valve review timelines, when to announce a date

### Takeaway
Hard minimums:
- The Coming Soon page must be public for at least 2 weeks before release.
- Valve's store page review and build review each take about 3 to 5 business days. Valve says to plan 7 business days for each.

So the absolute fastest path from today is roughly a 3.5 to 4 week launch. The marketing minimum is much longer, because wishlists accumulate while the page is live.

### Cited Findings
- "For new products, you must have a Coming Soon page up for at least two weeks before releasing." Submit the page for review at least 7 business days before you want it visible. At release, Steam automatically emails every wishlister — [Steamworks: Coming Soon](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/coming_soon)
- Store page review: typically 3 to 5 business days, so submit at least 7 business days ahead. Build review: also 3 to 5 business days, so plan at least 7 business days. Adult content can take longer — [Steamworks: Review Process](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/review_process)
- Set an exact date in Steamworks rather than a quarter. The date can be moved freely until about 14 days out — [howtomarketagame summary via search](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/02/14/whats-the-best-day-to-release-an-indie-game/) (secondary; verify against the Release Dates doc)
- Popular Upcoming eligibility takes roughly 5,000 to 7,000 wishlists — [howtomarketagame, 2026](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/02/18/the-counterintuitive-advantage-of-launching-during-steams-busiest-event/)

### Inferences
- Fastest legal Grenade Junky launch, computed:
  - Store page submitted about Oct 20, 2026.
  - Approved and set Coming Soon about Oct 27 to 29.
  - Two weeks of visibility puts the earliest launch about **Nov 11 to 12, 2026**. Submit the build in parallel.
  - That date still clears the Winter Sale cut-off (Nov 17) and the GTA VI date is irrelevant.
  - It leaves only about 2 weeks of wishlist collection, though, and no Next Fest.
- Timing the date announcement:
  - Announce the date once the game is on track for Popular Upcoming, or at the latest when it locks (about 14 days out).
  - For a Next Fest-then-launch plan, announce the date during the fest, while demo players are deciding whether to wishlist.

### Gaps
- No primary Valve source on the 14-day date-change lock. Not verified.
- No data in this research on the optimal Coming Soon duration (common practitioner advice is 6 to 12 months for an original indie). Not sourced here.

## 5. Spacing two launches from one studio: follower notifications, cross-promotion, bundles, sale participation

### Takeaway
No hard data on the optimal gap between launches. Steam's mechanics favor having Game 1 live and linked to a Creator Homepage well before Game 2:
- Followers get an email when Game 2 releases.
- The live game can join seasonal sales and themed fests that carry the studio's name.
- A bundle or "More from this developer" link cross-promotes the two.

### Cited Findings
- Creator Homepages: players "follow" the developer or publisher. Once Game 2's page goes Coming Soon it appears on the linked homepage automatically. At release it shows as a new release, and **followers get an email**. Each game must be linked to the homepage in admin — [Steamworks: Creator Homepage](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/creator_homepage)
- Followers are notified at launch only, not for discounts or demos. Use Steam news events to reach them otherwise — [howtomarketagame: wishlist vs follower](https://howtomarketagame.com/2023/05/01/what-is-the-difference-between-a-steam-wishlist-and-a-follower/)
- Steam now lets users follow the developer or publisher during purchase. Valve gave creator pages more visibility in May 2025 — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-47906); [GamingOnLinux](https://gamingonlinux.com/2025/05/valve-give-steam-developer-and-publisher-pages-a-little-extra-visibility)
- Steamworks also offers Franchise Pages and Developer, Publisher and Franchise sale pages as promo tools — [Steamworks marketing docs index](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/creatorhomepage)
- Seasonal sales are open to any released game (30-day rule). Themed fests also accept Coming Soon titles and demos, prioritizing discounted titles — [Steamworks: Upcoming Events](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events)

### Inferences
- Set up the Corrupted Media Creator Homepage **now** and link Grenade Junky. Every Grenade Junky buyer prompted to follow becomes a free launch-day email for PlayPen.
- Gap between the launches: 4 to 6 months is a reasonable judgment call, not a data finding. It leaves time to collect Grenade Junky lessons (pricing, Popular Upcoming behavior, review velocity, Next Fest demo conversion) before PlayPen's date locks, and keeps solo-dev support load from overlapping.
- Bundle: the audiences differ (VR boomer-shooter vs 2D puzzle-platformer), so a "complete the set" bundle is low value at PlayPen launch. It's more useful later as a studio sale page.
- Grenade Junky can't enter Next Fest after it releases. If it goes through Next Fest, its own launch comes after the fest. Both games each get one Next Fest, so the two games' fests should be different editions.

### Gaps
- No data on typical wishlist or sales uplift from developer follower emails.
- No evidence on optimal gaps between same-studio launches.
- Didn't confirm how "More from this developer" placement is chosen.

## 6. Early Access vs full launch, and recommended windows for both games

### Takeaway
Early Access counts as "released" for Next Fest eligibility, cooldowns and follower notifications:
- An EA launch uses up the Next Fest slot.
- An EA launch starts the 30-day discount cooldown.
- The "release" email to wishlisters and followers fires at EA launch, not at 1.0.

Neither game benefits much from EA:
- Grenade Junky is a finished port.
- PlayPen's systemic, content-driven design could suit EA. But its browser build already serves as the public test bed, and an EA launch would spend the one-time wishlist email on an unfinished art pass.

### Cited Findings
- Early Access launches are eligible for Next Fest until they launch. After that the game is released and ineligible — [Steamworks: Next Fest](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest)
- At release, Steam automatically emails wishlisters — [Steamworks: Coming Soon](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/coming_soon)
- Release cooldown: no discounts for 30 days after release — [Steamworks: Discounting](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/discounts)

### Inferences: recommended windows (all dates computed; weekday checked against the 2026 and 2027 calendars)

**Grenade Junky (PC VR, $10). Recommended plan A: Next Fest February 2027, then launch mid-April 2027.**
- Store page live (Coming Soon) by early November 2026, with a "Great on Frame" push. This collects wishlists through the Frame holiday season.
- Register for Next Fest Feb 2027 by **Jan 10, 2027**. Demo plus all items in by Feb 8. Demo live Feb 22 to Mar 1.
- Launch on **Mon Apr 12 or Wed Apr 14, 2027**:
  - Clear of the Spring Sale (Mar 18 to 25) and the busy March release peak.
  - About 6 weeks after Next Fest.
  - Clears the Summer Sale cut-off (May 25) by about 6 weeks, so Summer Sale (Jun 24) is its first big discount event.
- Launch discount 10% to 20% for 7 to 14 days.
- Why: it rehearses the full Next Fest playbook on the lower-stakes game before PlayPen. Next Fest gets UploadVR "featured PC VR demos" coverage. Mid-April is lighter than March. The Racing Fest (Apr 12 to 19) is irrelevant to genre.
- Risks: a 6-month wait for a game whose build is already uploaded. It's a Quest port, so possible Next Fest re-release or eligibility questions (it is new to Steam, so it should qualify — confirm with Valve). And it ties up the studio's Next Fest learnings on a VR title, whose demo dynamics differ from flat games.

**Grenade Junky plan B (faster): early February 2027, no Next Fest.**
- Page live in November 2026. Launch **Mon Feb 1 or Wed Feb 3, 2027**: quiet period, before Next Fest week (Feb 22).
- Eligible for the Spring Sale (cut-off Feb 16).
- Gets about 3 months of wishlist collection and catches post-holiday Steam Frame owners.

**Grenade Junky plan C (fastest legal): about Wed Nov 11 or Thu Nov 12, 2026.**
- Requires store page submission this week.
- Makes Winter Sale 2026 (cut-off about Nov 17) and Black Friday discount events.
- About 2 weeks of wishlists; heavy crunch risk.
- Only sensible if the main goal is to start learning ASAP and ride Frame holiday demand.

**PlayPen (2D comedic puzzle-platformer). Recommended: Next Fest June 2027 (Jun 14 to 21), then launch mid-to-late August 2027.**
- Steam Coming Soon page live as soon as key art from the overhaul is presentable (ideally by early 2027), linked to the Corrupted Media Creator Homepage.
- Next Fest June 2027 with a polished demo. Registration deadline will be about 5 to 6 weeks before (INFERRED from the Feb pattern).
- Launch **Mon Aug 16 or Wed Aug 18, 2027**:
